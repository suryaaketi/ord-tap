Sources and credits

ORD Tap is an ENGR 408 student prototype. It is not an official Chicago Department of Aviation service, and it is not affiliated with O'Hare International Airport.

Official references, checked November 4, 2026

  • O'Hare interactive map (Chicago Department of Aviation, built on Atrius). Used to verify gate labels, concourse shapes, amenity locations, levels, the B/C pedestrian tunnel and its escalators and elevators, and walking distances for Terminal 1.
  • Official printable terminal maps, including the Terminal 1 map (Upper Level, Lower Level, Pedestrian Tunnel Level).
  • Terminal accessibility and amenities: all-gender restrooms at C19, nursing rooms, service animal relief areas.
  • Transportation between terminals: Terminal Transfer Bus stops and hours, the ATS rule, and secure walkways between Terminals 1, 2 and 3.

Open map data

  • Airport boundary, terminal and concourse building outlines, and runways: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License. Retrieved November 4, 2026 through the Overpass API and simplified for the phone map (data/outline.json).

Flight information

The default flight board embeds Avionio’s O’Hare widget for departures and arrivals. With a Cloudflare Worker configured, the native board and flight lookup use the Aviationstack API. Confirm your gate with your airline or O’Hare’s official flight status. Sample flights are available in demo mode.

What is original

The gate positions, walking network, labels and route drawing are original work; building outlines come from OpenStreetMap. Gates were placed from the official map, not traced from airport drawings. The walking network for Terminal 1 is a small hand-built set of corridor lines; its distances were compared with the official map's routes and agree within about 3%.

Known differences between sources

The Terminal 1 PDF says to transfer to Terminal 5 at Gate B1. The transportation page says the Gate B1 stop is temporarily closed for construction and the bus boards at Gate C18B. This prototype follows the transportation page.

Coverage

Terminal 1 (Concourses B and C, the B/C tunnel): routes. Terminals 2, 3 and 5: map preview only. Their gates and amenities are placed from the official map, but walking routes have not been verified yet.
